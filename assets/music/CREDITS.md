## Notes on `tracks.json`

`seconds` comes from the file itself. `bpm` and `startAt` are hints, not source data: `bpm` is the
artist's stated tempo for Neon Laser Horizon and a measured estimate for the others; `startAt` is the
measured second at which the full arrangement is playing (after a fade-in or build-up).
For the tracks added later the same day (Touch The Sky onwards in `tracks.json`), `bpm` is an estimate
from an onset autocorrelation (Space City: from the file name; Voltaic: the artist's stated tempo) and
`startAt` is the measured second at which the bass and drums are in (Voltaic: the artist's description
says the piece gets going 16 seconds in).
