## Notes on `tracks.json`

`seconds` comes from the file itself. `bpm` and `startAt` are hints, not source data: `bpm` is the
artist's stated tempo for Neon Laser Horizon and a measured estimate for the others; `startAt` is the
measured second at which the full arrangement is playing (after a fade-in or build-up).
