# Fungi assets — credits and licences

Third-party low-poly mushroom models in `assets/fungi/`. Every pack was fetched on 2026-11-16 with plain anonymous downloads from the source page listed, and the licence below is the one stated on that page (or in the pack's own licence file) on that date.

Only CC0 / public-domain and CC-BY (attribution only) material is included. CC-BY models must stay credited (this file, plus the game's credits screen or README). `manifest.json` lists every model with its pack, triangle count, licence, author and source, most toadstool-like first; creatures and props come last.

Nothing was converted or edited: every file is byte-for-byte the downloaded file. Poly Pizza files were renamed `<title>-<id>.glb`. All 26 files are glTF 2.0 and self-contained (no external `.bin` or texture files).
