# Flora assets — credits and licences

Third-party flower, plant and tree assets in `assets/flora/`. Every pack was fetched on 2026-11-16 from the source page listed, and the licence below is the one stated on that page on that date.

Only CC0 / public-domain and CC-BY (attribution only) material is included. CC-BY packs must stay credited (this file, plus the game's credits screen or README). `manifest.json` lists every sprite and model with its rectangle, style, kind and pack.

Changes made to the originals are stated per pack (background made transparent, files renamed, subsets taken). Everything else is byte-for-byte the downloaded file.
