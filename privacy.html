Privacy, and deleting your data

Palm Whorl Cities is a game in the browser. This page says what it keeps about you and how to remove it.

What is kept

  • On your device: a random device token, your Hand's map DNA, what you charted and pinned, and your seed pouch.
  • On the shared map: the hash of that token; your Hand (its map DNA, place and the name you gave it) if you laid one; the groves you planted (where, which species, when; not who you are); the names you gave places; and your seed pouch (seeds, herbarium, sowing log), so it survives a cleared browser.
  • If you sign in with Facebook: a hash of the app-scoped user id Facebook gives this game, used only to give all your devices the same seed pouch. The game asks for your public profile permission only and does not read or keep your name, e-mail address, picture or friends.

Never kept: photos of your hands, hand-tracking data, your position in your room, or any advertising or analytics data. There are no trackers on the site.

Deleting your data

  1. Open the game's first page on the device you played on.
  2. Choose Forget my Hand at the bottom. This removes your Hand, its place names and its groves from the shared map, deletes your seed pouch and the Facebook link from the map, and clears the game's data on that device.
  3. If you signed in with Facebook, you can also remove the game under Facebook → Settings → Apps and websites.

If you no longer have the device, write to the owner through the game's page on GitHub and say which Facebook account you used; the linked data will be removed.
